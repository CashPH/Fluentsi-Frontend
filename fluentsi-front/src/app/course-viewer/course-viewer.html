TAG QUESTIONS

About Lesson

A tag question is a small question that we add to the end of a statement. Instead of asking a full question, we use them to confirm information or to see if the other person agrees with us.

1. The Golden Rule of Polarity

The most important rule to remember is that the tag is always the opposite of the statement:

  • Positive Statement (+) → Negative Tag (-)

    Example: You are a student, aren't you?

  • Negative Statement (-) → Positive Tag (+)

    Example: You aren't a doctor, are you?

2. Finding the Right Auxiliary Verb

The tag question must use the same tense and the same auxiliary verb as the main sentence. If there is no auxiliary verb (like in Present or Past Simple), we use do / does / did.
